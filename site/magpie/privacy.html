Magpie

Privacy policy

Magpie personal backup tool. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Magpie is a private backup tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers and Google account.

What Magpie accesses

With the owner's permission, Magpie connects to the owner's Google Drive to upload encrypted backup archives, and to list and delete the archives it uploaded earlier. It asks only for access to files it created itself (Google's drive.file permission).

What Magpie collects

Nothing. Magpie has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The backup archives go straight from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive and home file server, and they are encrypted before they leave the computer.

Sharing

Magpie doesn't share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. Google user data is used only to store and manage the owner's own backups, and for nothing else.

Storage and removal

The Google sign-in token is kept on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the backup archives can be deleted from Google Drive directly.

This website

This site is a few static pages, delivered through Cloudflare. The pages themselves set no cookies and run no scripts.
